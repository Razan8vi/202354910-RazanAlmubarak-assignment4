# User Guide

A short guide to everything a visitor can do on my portfolio.

**Live site:** https://razan8vi.github.io/202354910-RazanAlmubarak-assignment4/

## 1. Top bar

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **About / Projects / GitHub / Contact** | Scrolls to that section |
| **العربية** / **English** | Switches the whole site between English and Arabic (Arabic reads right to left). Your choice is remembered |
| **☀ Light mode** / **☾ Dark mode** | Switches the colors. Your choice is remembered |

## 2. Greeting and your name

- The greeting changes with the time of day.
- Type your first name under **What should I call you?** and press **Save**.
  The site greets you by name, counts your visits, and fills your name in the
  contact form.
- **Not you? Change name** removes the saved name.

## 3. Show or hide sections

Every section (About, Projects, GitHub) has a **Hide** / **Show** button.
Hidden sections stay hidden the next time you visit.

## 4. Projects

1. Pick a **category**: All, Web, Mobile, or Software Engineering.
2. Pick a **level**: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced. A short message
   describes each level.
3. Type in **Search** to find words in titles, descriptions, or tags (English
   or Arabic).
4. Choose **Sort by**: newest, oldest, or title.

The counter shows how many projects match. If nothing matches, the message
says which filters to change, and **Reset filters** brings everything back.
Your category, level, and sort choices are remembered.

## 5. Latest on GitHub

My newest repositories load live from GitHub. If they cannot load (no
internet, GitHub busy), a message explains why; press **Try again**.

## 6. Contact form

1. Fill in your name, email, and a reason.
2. If you choose **Project collaboration**, you can add a project link
   (optional, must start with `https://`).
3. Write a message of 10–500 characters; the counter shows how many you used.
4. Each field shows a message if something is wrong. **Send Message** turns on
   when everything is correct, and the text under it lists what is missing.

The form shows a confirmation, but it does not send a real email (no server
is connected).

## 7. Your flower (digital garden)

The flower pot in the corner (bottom-left in English, bottom-right in Arabic)
grows as you scroll down the page. There is nothing to click.

| Where you are | What you see |
|---|---|
| Top of the page | A seed in the soil |
| Scrolling down | The stem grows, then two leaves |
| Past the middle | A bud appears and slowly opens |
| Bottom of the page | Full bloom, a little dance, and a thank-you message |

- The small label on the pot shows how far down you are (for example `45%`).
- Scroll back up and the flower gets smaller again.

## 8. Timer

The footer shows how long you have been on the site. It keeps counting if you
reload the page.

## 9. Resetting everything

To clear every saved choice (theme, language, name, filters), open the
browser's developer tools → **Application** → **Local Storage**, and delete the
keys that start with `portfolio.`.
